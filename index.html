GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you begin.

A few useful things to know before discussing your project with Tarangan Associates.

What services does Tarangan Associates provide?+

The business offers architecture and planning, civil construction, interior and exterior design, 2D/3D design and related project consultation. Confirm the exact scope for your project directly.

Do you provide 2D and 3D architectural design?+

2D and 3D home design are among the services described in the supplied business information. Share your site details and requirements to discuss deliverables.

Can I discuss a bungalow or residential construction project?+

Yes. Contact the team to discuss the location, plot or built-up area, preferred design direction and intended scope.

Do you provide interior design and modular kitchen planning?+

Interior design is part of the supplied service profile. Discuss room requirements, measurements, material preferences and the level of design or execution support you need.

Can you discuss commercial or institutional buildings?+

You can enquire about commercial and institutional project requirements. Specialist engineering, statutory approvals and professional sign-off must be confirmed for each project.

Do you undertake turnkey projects?

Turnkey and project coordination requirements can be discussed. The responsibilities, inclusions, exclusions and execution scope should be documented before work begins.

How can I estimate my interior design budget?

Use the calculator on this page as an illustrative planning tool. Its demonstration rates are not official prices. A reliable estimate needs measurements, material specifications and a confirmed scope.

How do I contact Tarangan Associates?

Call +91 98607 73107, send a WhatsApp enquiry or use the project consultation form on this website.
